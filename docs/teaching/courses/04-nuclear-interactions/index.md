# 04 Nuclear Interactions

This course is a stand-alone module on nuclear reactions in ion transport.

## Audience

Students who need to understand nuclear attenuation, fragments, neutrons, and
model comparisons in proton or ion transport.

## Learning goals

After this course, students should be able to:

- identify signatures of nuclear reactions in depth-dose and fluence curves;
- compare primary attenuation and fragment production;
- distinguish charged fragments from neutron transport;
- interpret model-comparison plots with appropriate statistical caution.
