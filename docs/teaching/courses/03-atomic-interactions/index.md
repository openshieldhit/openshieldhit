# 03 Atomic Interactions

This course focuses on charged-particle interactions with atomic electrons and
nuclei treated as continuous or condensed transport effects.

## Audience

Students studying stopping power, range, straggling, and multiple scattering.

## Learning goals

After this course, students should be able to:

- connect stopping power to range and depth-dose curves;
- describe energy-loss straggling qualitatively;
- identify when multiple scattering matters;
- compare material and projectile dependencies.
